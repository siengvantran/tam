# TAM.TV — Development Brief (summary)

> Build TAM.TV as a location-independent live music and media platform that uses TAM Festival events to discover and develop emerging artists, capture authentic stories and performances, distribute content across social/search/AI channels, build a first-party audience and progressively use audience data and AI to drive future programming and growth.

## Proposition
*Imagine if MTV had a live music venue, but focused on emerging artists.*

Artists are discovered → programmed → performed → filmed → interviewed → published → discovered again. The live event creates the raw material; TAM.TV turns it into lasting media; social distributes it; search and AI discovery bring new audiences; audience data informs what TAM does next.

The objective is not to digitise the venue. It is to make TAM location-independent while keeping the live music experience at its heart. The Elephant & Castle site is the launchpad for TAM's next form.

## Core principle
**One event should generate an ecosystem of content**: full performance video, 3–10 clips, interview, backstage, photo gallery, written profile, radio segment, playlist inclusion, platform-specific social posts, a searchable artist page, links to the artist's music, future opportunities, engagement data.

## Interfaces into one ecosystem
Live · TV · Radio · Magazine · Social · Community · Data · AI

## Content pillars
Live · Artists · People · Projects (e.g. Dana's documentary) · Music · Festival · Stories. Editorial philosophy: **Real. Immediate. Human.** The transition itself is part of the story.

## Principles
- TAM.TV is the source of truth; social platforms are distribution. *Create once, distribute everywhere*, and everything links back.
- Structured pages and metadata for every artist, event, performance and story: a growing TAM knowledge graph for search and AI.
- First-party audience: follower → visitor → registered → member → ticket buyer → repeat participant.
- Consent, privacy and UK/EU GDPR designed in from day one. Use data to make TAM more relevant, not just to advertise.

## Risks to avoid
Building everything at once · content without audience · technology too early · membership before value · platform dependence.

## Phases
1. Digital home (this MVP) · 2. TAM Festival ecosystem · 3. "Play TAM" submissions · 4. Audience participation / pledges · 5. £10 membership · 6. AI-assisted programming.

## Key metric
**Is the TAM audience growing independently of the physical venue?**
